HDM CRM Privacy Notice

1. Who handles your data

High Demand Media provides HDM CRM. We manage account, security and service information to operate the product. Each customer organization controls the contacts, companies, tasks, tickets, notes and other business records it places in its workspace. We process that customer data to deliver its contracted services and according to its instructions. Contact info@highdemandmedia.com.

2. Information processed

The CRM processes invited users’ names, email addresses, profile preferences, organization details, permissions, business records, attachments, activity history, integration credentials and linked communications. We record the version, time, language, user and organization when terms are accepted. Permissions determine record access within an organization. Support access must be limited to what is authorized and necessary.

3. Purposes and boundaries

We use customer data only to operate, secure, support and deliver the services that customer has contracted. We do not sell customer CRM data, use it for our own advertising campaigns or make it available for advertising based on connected Google data. Organizations are responsible for their own lawful collection, notices and instructions. A customer’s website form must provide that customer’s privacy information.

4. Google integrations

Google sign-in uses your verified email and profile information to identify your invited CRM account and populate your profile. HDM does not receive your Google password. Connecting Gmail or Google Calendar is a separate, optional choice per user. Before connecting, the CRM explains the data access and asks you to continue to Google’s consent screen.

Gmail synchronization inspects message headers to match email addresses to your accessible CRM contacts, then reads and stores matching message text and metadata, including sender, recipients, subject, timestamps and message/thread identifiers. Initial import covers up to the last 90 days. Google Gmail change notifications, delivered through Google Cloud Pub/Sub, trigger background synchronization; scheduled synchronization remains a recovery fallback. The notifications identify the mailbox and its change history rather than carrying message bodies. This is an import window, not an automatic 90-day deletion deadline. The CRM can send, reply to and forward emails when you instruct it. Message content, recipients and any files you attach are sent to Google for delivery to the recipients you select. Attachment files and remote tracking images are not imported by this synchronization. Mailbox visibility is restricted to its connected user; internal comments can be visible to colleagues with access to the associated record.

Calendar access lists your calendars and reads event details from your connected calendar to display your schedule and synchronize linked CRM appointments, including titles, start/end times, guest email addresses, invitation responses, changes and cancellations. Guest information is sent to Google to deliver invitations to the selected guests. Additional calendars you select for availability are queried for busy periods; their event titles and descriptions are not imported by that availability check. Internal CRM meeting notes and contact notes are not sent to Google or guests. Google descriptions may be displayed for imported Google events. Google change notifications trigger background updates; scheduled synchronization remains a fallback, and delivery is not guaranteed to be instantaneous.

Google data is processed by the CRM on Render infrastructure; connections to Google use HTTPS, and credentials and cached message bodies are encrypted by the application. Access controls restrict private mailbox data to the connected user. Relevant integration data may remain while the connection is authorized and active. Gmail, Google Calendar and Google sign-in use separate Google applications. Disconnecting Gmail or Calendar requests revocation of that service’s Google authorization and removes its local credentials and cached integration data from your current profile, without revoking the other service or Google sign-in. Revocation may also affect that same service connected with the same Google account in other CRM profiles; those profiles clear their private cache when they detect the revoked authorization. If Google cannot process revocation, the CRM reports the failure so you can retry. You may also revoke permissions directly in your Google Account. Original Google messages and events, independent CRM records and internal comments are not deleted by disconnecting. Residual backups follow the retention described below.

Use and transfer of raw or derived Google user data follow the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. We do not sell this data, use it for ad targeting or retargeting, train general AI/ML models with it, or transfer it to third-party AI/ML services. Google integrations do not provide cold-email or email-warming features.

Transfers of Google data are limited to providing these user-facing features with your consent, necessary security investigations or legal obligations. Human access to Google data requires your documented consent to the specific data, or a necessary security or legal purpose. General CRM support access does not grant access to your private Gmail mailbox.

5. Providers and processing locations

The service uses Render for application infrastructure, Amazon S3 for file storage and Amazon Simple Email Service (SES) for transactional email. Google provides optional sign-in and connected Gmail and Calendar integrations. Authorized infrastructure providers process data to deliver these functions. Hosting and processing can involve the United States and other locations supported by providers; no exclusive country or region is guaranteed in this notice.

Render

Application hosting and background processing for CRM requests and scheduled jobs.

Amazon Web Services (AWS)

Amazon S3 stores CRM files. Amazon SES processes recipient addresses, message content and delivery information to send CRM invitations, account recovery messages, notifications, appointment notices and quote confirmations. These system emails are separate from your connected Gmail mailbox.

Google

Google identity for optional sign-in and Gmail and Google Calendar APIs when you connect them. Gmail delivers messages you choose to send from your connected mailbox; it is not the provider for CRM system emails. Google also provides the underlying Google accounts under its own terms.

Website-specific providers

Hostinger hosts the marketing site. Quote requests are processed by HDM CRM and its transactional email sender. Optional Google and Meta tags concern website tracking and are not part of the authenticated CRM. They are not authorized to receive connected Gmail data for advertising.

Changes and questions

Contact info@highdemandmedia.com for questions about processing locations or providers. This list describes the reviewed architecture, not a guarantee that all processing occurs in a single country. Material provider changes require review and customer notice as described in the agreement.

6. Retention and service closure

During service, data remains available according to permissions and the customer’s instructions. When the organization owner or HDM CRM owner starts closure under the service agreement, the organization has 30 days of read-only access for consultation and export. New records and integration synchronization stop. At the end of those 30 days, access and the option to cancel closure end permanently. Active workspace data is scheduled for deletion; HDM will not recover or restore the closed organization’s data. Any residual backup copies are not available for customer recovery. Storage cleanup may retry after provider failures. Backup copies are subject to the actual provider backup lifecycle and must not be restored to active use without reapplying deletion requests. Limited records may be retained where law requires or for necessary legal claims.

7. Your choices and requests

You can update profile preferences, disconnect integrations and ask your organization administrator about your workspace data. Contact the organization that collected your information for access, correction, export or deletion requests. HDM assists that organization under the applicable agreement and law. For your HDM account information or a privacy complaint, contact info@highdemandmedia.com. We may proportionately verify your identity; available rights depend on applicable law.

In Notifications → Preferences you can enable or disable Pop-up, Browser, Bell and Email for the supported topics in your current organization. The CRM stores those selections with your organization profile and saves changes automatically. Turning a channel off does not delete existing history or recall delivered emails. Account recovery, account invitations and guest meeting invitations are separate service communications; these controls are not marketing consent. Browser alerts require permission on that device and an open CRM tab. They show an alert topic without record names or comment text. Browser and operating-system settings can affect delivery. Pop-up previews, Bell history and notification emails can contain relevant record names, dates or comment excerpts, subject to recipient access checks.

Light or Dark appearance and list display preferences are saved in browser storage rather than synchronized across devices. Notification delivery receipts store identifiers and timestamps locally to reduce repeated alerts across tabs; message bodies and comment text are not stored in those receipts. Clearing browser site data can reset these local choices. Browser notification permission is managed separately in your browser. Necessary authentication cookies support sign-in; Remember me for 14 days allows a maximum fourteen-day session, while an unchecked choice uses session cookies and an eight-hour maximum. Renewal does not extend that deadline. Use Sign out on shared devices because browser session restoration can preserve session cookies.

8. Security and updates

The service uses authentication, organization boundaries, permissions and safeguards appropriate to its functions. No system is entirely risk-free. Do not upload regulated health, payment-card or other specially restricted data unless a separate written agreement expressly supports it. Material changes receive an updated version and notice where required. This notice does not authorize unrelated marketing or replace Google’s consent screen.
