# Deal stages

Display order: Prospecting, Proposal, Follow Up, Stan By, Close Won, Close Lost.

Existing storage identifiers are retained for compatibility with deal history, aging settings and integrations: QUALIFICATION now displays Follow Up; NEGOTIATION displays Stan By. CLOSED_WON and CLOSED_LOST retain their closed-deal rules. Existing records retain their stage IDs and entry timestamps. This change does not alter probability or aging thresholds.
